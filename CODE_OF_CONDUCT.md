# Community conduct

Prabha welcomes respectful participation from people of every background and level of experience. Use considerate language, credit sources, offer constructive technical feedback and respect others' time and privacy.

Harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats, doxxing, spam and knowingly misleading research claims are unacceptable. Maintainers may edit or remove offending content, close threads, or limit participation proportionately. Explain moderation decisions when doing so does not expose private information.

Report public conduct problems through GitHub's reporting tools. For private or security-sensitive matters, use the repository's available private reporting channel. Do not disclose another person's personal information in a public issue.
